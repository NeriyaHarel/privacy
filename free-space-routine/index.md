# Free Space Routine — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-11-23

Free Space Routine ("the app") helps you keep your phone tidy: it finds duplicate and look-alike photos, junk files and
old files, and asks you whether to remove them. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## The short version

- Everything the app does happens **on your phone**. Your photos, videos, files, file names, faces and answers are
  **never sent to us or to anyone else**.
- The app has **no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission** in this version.
- Removing something moves it to a **trash inside the app** for 30 days, so you can bring it back.
- Uninstalling the app deletes the app's own data.

## What the app can see

- **All files access.** Android asks you to allow this permission. The app uses it to look through your shared
  storage for photos, videos, documents, installers, temporary files and empty folders, to show what uses your space,
  and to move files you approve to the app's trash. It reads file contents only to compare files (for example to find
  exact copies), on the phone, and it does not look into other apps' private folders.
- **Photos and videos.** The app reads your photos to compare them (to find bursts, duplicates and look-alikes),
  to judge sharpness, and to find faces. This work is done on the phone.
- **Notifications** (optional). If you turn on reminders, the app asks Android for permission to show them.

## What the app stores on your phone

All of this stays in the app's private storage on your phone:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Small thumbnails, colour summaries and hashes of your photos | To compare photos quickly without re-reading them every time |
| Where faces are in each photo (position, smile / eyes open estimates) | To choose the best shot of a series |
| **Face signatures ("embeddings")** | To group photos of the same person in the **People** tab. A face signature is a list of numbers computed from a face. It is a biometric-style identifier, so we say it clearly: it is created and kept **only on your phone**, never uploaded, and not used to identify anyone outside the app |
| Names you give to people, your Keep / Delete answers and your settings | To remember your choices |
| The app's trash folder (`.FreeSpaceRoutineTrash` on your shared storage) and a list of what is in it | So removed files can be restored for 30 days, then they are deleted for good |

You can delete the People data by removing people or albums in the app. Uninstalling the app removes the app's
private data; the trash folder on shared storage is removed by the app when you empty the trash (anything still in it
when you uninstall stays in that folder until you delete it).

## Face detection and Google Play services

To find faces the app uses the on-device face model from **Google Play services (ML Kit)**. The model runs on your
phone; photos are not sent anywhere. Google Play services downloads and updates the model itself, like any other
Google component, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app itself does not
connect to the internet.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
- We do not use advertising or analytics services, and we do not track you.
- We do not upload photos, files, file names, folder names, faces or your answers.
- The app is not meant for children under 13 (or the age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect
  anything from children.

## Your control

- You decide what is removed: nothing is removed without your answer, except the kinds you have turned on
  "auto clean" for, and those can be turned off at any time. Everything removed can be restored for 30 days.
- You can revoke All files access or notifications at any time in Android settings; the app then cannot scan.
- Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on a server. To delete the app's
  data, clear its storage in Android settings or uninstall it.

## Changes

If a later version adds something that touches your data (for example ads or purchases), we will update this page
first and say so in the app. The date at the top shows the latest change.
